PART 1: DAVID SHAPIRO — POST-LABOR ECONOMICS (PLE)
================================================================================

Core Premise & Diagnosis:
• The Breakdown of Bilateral Dependence: Throughout history, civil rights and democratic accountability existed because the state and capital had a "double bilateral dependence" on ordinary citizens: they needed citizens as laborers for economic production and as soldiers for military defense. In return, citizens demanded political franchise, legal protection, and public services.
• The Technofeudalism Threat: When AI and autonomous robotics replace human labor and automate military defense, this bilateral dependence dissolves. Capital and the state no longer require the populace. If left unaddressed, the default trajectory is "technofeudalism" or a "rentier state," where a small techno-oligarchy controls the means of production and the majority becomes an economically superfluous population.
• Critique of Pure Cash UBI: Traditional Universal Basic Income (UBI) or Negative Income Tax (NIT) funded from annual legislative budgets is a fragile stopgap, not a permanent solution. It turns citizens into "financial outpatients" dependent on the shifting whims of politicians. An annual budget line can be slashed, means-tested, or conditioned on behavioral compliance, leaving citizens with zero structural leverage.

Core Mechanism & Architecture:
• Universal Basic Capital (UBC): Rather than merely redistributing income, society must "refactor capitalism" by distributing capital ownership directly to citizens, giving everyone an independent income stream derived from asset appreciation and machine productivity.
• Multi-Tiered Public Wealth Funds:
  1. Sovereign Wealth Funds (National): Modeled on Norway's Government Pension Fund Global (GPFG), funded through resource extraction rents, corporate equity contributions, spectrum auctions, and automation levies.
  2. Regional / State Funds: Modeled on the Alaska Permanent Fund and New Mexico's Land Grant Permanent Fund.
  3. Municipal / Urban Wealth Funds: Modeled on Singapore's Temasek, holding local infrastructure, public land, and municipal utilities.
• Complementary Ownership On-Ramps:
  - Baby Bonds: Federally seeded, compounding capital endowments granted to every newborn.
  - Employee Ownership Trusts (EOTs) & ESOPs: Converting private corporations into worker-owned trusts so enterprise automation gains accrue to workers.
  - Cooperatives & DAOs: Legal vehicles for decentralized community ownership of automated facilities.
• Institutional Shielding: Public wealth funds must adhere to the Santiago Principles (international standards of transparency, independent auditing, strict withdrawal formulas, and insulation from legislative meddling).
• Subsidiarity & Human Agency: Pushing economic decisions to the lowest effective municipal level to prevent central state monopolization.

PART 2: EMAD MOSTAQUE — THE LAST ECONOMY / UNIVERSAL BASIC AI (UBAI)
================================================================================

Core Premise & Diagnosis:
• The Intelligence Inversion: Historically, intelligence and energy were scarce; raw materials were priced based on the human labor required to extract and refine them. AI turns intelligence into an abundant, non-metabolic utility with near-zero marginal cost of reproduction.
• The Abundance Trap: Modern economic metrics (GDP, corporate revenue) only measure scarcity. When AI performs medical diagnosis, coding, legal drafting, and tutoring for pennies, GDP reads the collapse in service costs as an economic contraction rather than an explosion in living standards.
• The Fiscal Implosion of the Welfare State: Western welfare states are funded almost exclusively by payroll taxes and personal income taxes on human wages. When AI and humanoid robotics collapse the economic value of human labor, income-tax receipts evaporate at the exact moment social safety-net demands spike. Traditional cash UBI funded by income taxes is an arithmetic impossibility.
• The Three Attractor States:
  1. Digital Feudalism (The Default): Mega-tech monopolies own proprietary frontier models; citizens receive minimal subsistence welfare ("enough to survive, not enough to matter"), remaining passive consumers.
  2. The Great Fragmentation: Nations panic over job loss, nationalizing AI behind digital iron curtains and engaging in mercantilist cyberwar.
  3. Human Symbiosis (The Goal): Compute, energy, and open-source models are chartered as a universal public utility.

Core Mechanism & Architecture:
• Universal Basic AI (UBAI): Compute cycles, clean energy, and open foundation model weights are chartered as public utilities, equivalent to water, roads, and electricity.
• The Mechanism: Every citizen receives an unconditional monthly voucher for:
  1. Compute capacity (GPU / FLOP hours).
  2. Energy credits to run local inferencing.
  3. Open-source model weights (preventing censorship or corporate lock-in).
• "Minting Value" vs. Passive Consumption: Citizens use their compute allocation and personal AI agents to solve local community problems, conduct scientific research, build decentralized network businesses, and coordinate local capital.
• Alternative Accounting: Replacing GDP with the "Four Capitals" (MIND framework: Material, Intellectual, Natural, and Digital/Social capital).

PART 3: ROB BROWN — THE ROBOT DIVIDEND (SUMMARY & FULL TEXT)
================================================================================

Summary:
Brown grounds his model in thermodynamic materialism: wealth is the physical output produced by energy, machines, and raw materials; money is merely an unforgeable accounting ticket. Deprivation amid automated abundance is an accounting failure, not a physical scarcity. 

Rather than attempting to amass an impossible capital stock endowment ($124T+ at national scale), Brown's model taxes current commercial flows: an algorithmic Negative Income Tax (NIT) schedule (modeled by dials T and P) bounded within a statutory Capacity Corridor [T_min, T_max] that guarantees industrial capital replenishment before dividend extraction. 

Funding Engine:
1. Destination-Based Cash-Flow Taxation (DBCFT / border-adjusted VAT) assessed at retail consumption with 100% immediate CapEx expensing, mathematically isolating economic rent and neutralizing tax havens without subjective deduction loopholes.
2. Georgist Land Value Taxation (LVT) on unimproved urban locations, alongside royalties on raw energy throughput and mineral extraction, preventing landlords from capturing dividends into higher rents.
3. Terrestrial Benchmark (US Scale): At $28T–$30T GDP, a 15% DBCFT ($2.85T) + 5% LVT ($1.0T) + Resource Royalties ($600B) generates $4.45 trillion annually, funding an unconditional baseline dividend of $17,100 per adult per year ($34,200 for a two-adult household) without debt or capital erosion.

Strategic & Transition Dynamics:
• Transforming the Dilemma: Automation in an unregulated market is a Prisoner's Dilemma, but statutory implementation transforms it into an Assurance Game (Stag Hunt) where the Robot Dividend is the unique subgame-perfect Nash equilibrium.
• The Realization Trap & Minimum Efficient Scale: Disproves elite autarky using Adam Smith's division of labor: frontier hardware (ASML EUV fabs, gigawatt datacenters, humanoid robotics) requires mass consumer volume to amortize fixed capital costs. Capital cannot survive in an insular enclave.
• The Anti-War Abundance Mobilization: Contrast with WWII borrowing (which financed physical destruction); transition bonds finance expanding, permanent physical productivity inside a deflating cost structure.
• The All-Volunteer Labor Clearing: Physical trades clear via compensating wage differentials funded by corporate payroll savings, mirroring the post-1973 all-volunteer military.
• Political Leverage Tipping Point: Enacted mid-transition at 25–35% displacement when middle-class electoral leverage peaks.
