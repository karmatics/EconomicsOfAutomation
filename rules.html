Epistemological Framework

9. Methodological Ground Rules

The four analytical constraints required to evaluate post-labor macroeconomic models without falling into performative cynicism or accounting fallacies.

Analytical Axioms
The Four Axiomatic Constraints of First-Principles Economics

1. The Behavioral Consistency Axiom: Game-theoretic actors must be modeled as rational utility-maximizers. Dystopian scenarios requiring actors to choose cartoon genocide over compounding wealth fail the consistency test.

2. Structural Rules vs. Tactical Evasion: Differentiate between micro-level tax avoidance and macro-level constitutional support for consumer purchasing power.

3. Closed-Economy General Equilibrium: Planetary macroeconomics is a closed system. Models cannot externalize realization crises to an imaginary outside sink.

4. Mechanical Falsifiability: Critics asserting that a universal dividend will fail must specify an alternative, mathematically balanceable equilibrium that does not violate physical accounting identities.

When evaluating post-labor macroeconomic transitions, public and academic discussions frequently derail into unexamined cynicism or cognitive dissonance. Conditioned by thousands of years of biological scarcity, human intuition reflexively treats work as the moral price of admission to survival, assuming that an automated economy without mandatory toil must inevitably collapse into tyranny or starvation.

To separate rigorous institutional engineering from cinematic tropes, economic proposals must be stress-tested against formal game theory and physical thermodynamics. The four methodological standards establish the analytical guardrails necessary to evaluate this treatise on first principles.
