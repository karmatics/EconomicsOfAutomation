Skeptics often counter: “What if elites don't hire a human 10% Praetorian Guard at all? If AI and robotics automate defense, won't capital owners simply deploy autonomous weapon systems and killer drones to enforce total disenfranchisement?”

This dystopian trope collapses under two physical and strategic realities:

  1. Asymmetric Sabotage & Industrial Fragility: Autonomous weapon systems are not self-sustaining sci-fi monoliths; they are delicate, highly maintenance-intensive physical hardware. Drones, autonomous sentries, and robotic surveillance platforms require continuous charging grids, clean sub-millimeter optical sensors, sophisticated hydraulic maintenance, specialized cooling, and continuous microelectronic supply chains. In a hostile population of 300 million, an automated defense perimeter is physically indefensible against asymmetric electronic warfare, fiber-optic line cuts, optical laser blinding, physical trap networks, and infrastructure sabotage. An elite cannot operate a high-tech supply chain from inside an armed camp under permanent siege.
  2. The Cryptographic Coup Dilemma: Who holds the root administrative keys, firmware signing certificates, and neural policy updates for an autonomous defense grid? In an autocracy where the rule of law has been dismantled, the autocrat does not command the drones directly—the software architecture does. Relying purely on algorithmic force creates catastrophic single-point-of-failure coup risk: whoever controls the root server, writes the model updates, or controls the power switch instantly becomes the absolute sovereign ruler. Autocrats throughout history park their wealth in rule-of-law democracies because private force makes the wealthy the immediate hostages of their own enforcement machinery.

Sharing surplus through the Robot Dividend is the Bismarckian Insurance Equilibrium: paying an unconditional dividend costs a tiny fraction of the catastrophic, compounding expense of attempting to secure an industrial empire against domestic collapse.

To understand why rational self-interest leads to constitutional support for the dividend, we must use standard macroeconomic methodology: a Closed-Economy General Equilibrium Framework.

In neoclassical economics, when evaluating planetary systems, economists assume a closed model because the Earth has no trading partners in outer space; global imports must equal global exports, and global production must match global realization. In a closed general equilibrium model, capital cannot externalize its realization crisis.

Where does enlightened self-interest enter? In the distinction between tactical tax compliance and constitutional support. At the micro-tactical level, every firm attempts to minimize its private tax bill. But at the macro-constitutional level, rational capital owners support the legal auto-pilot that taxes machine surplus to keep domestic consumers solvent.

Public goods are often under-provided when actors can free-ride. But Destination-Based Cash-Flow Taxation eliminates the free-rider loophole by removing subjective deductions and assessing tax at the domestic register. Capital owners comply because statutory law coerces them, and they support the legal framework because an automated economy without solvent domestic customers is an engine running with no transmission.
